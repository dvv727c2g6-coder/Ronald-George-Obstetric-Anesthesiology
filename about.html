Ronald B. George, MD FRCPC

Obstetric anesthesiologist, researcher, educator and academic leader.

Biography

Dr. Ronald B. George is a Professor of Anesthesiology at the University of Toronto and Director of Obstetric Anesthesiology at Mount Sinai Hospital, Toronto. His work brings together clinical research, quality improvement, patient-centred care, and national and international education.

His academic interests include the effectiveness and experience of labour analgesia, cesarean delivery and recovery, postpartum hemorrhage, and equitable access to obstetric anesthesia services.

Education and training

MD, Dalhousie University; obstetric anesthesia clinical and research fellowship, Duke University Medical Center; Fellow of the Royal College of Physicians of Canada.
